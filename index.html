🌟 SOLIDGROUND

"See your future before inflation changes it."

Helping users make smarter financial decisions through inflation tracking, budgeting tools, and future spending predictions.

🎯 Pitch

SolidGround helps users understand how inflation affects their spending, savings, and future finances through budgeting tools and personalized predictions.

🌍 Theme Implementation

Our app uses inflation tracking, spending analysis, and future projections to help users make smarter financial decisions before prices rise.

🧠 Why We Built This

We built SolidGround to make financial literacy simple, interactive, and useful for people navigating an unpredictable economy.

Our goal is to help users save money by providing budgeting tools, showing lower-cost products, and offering guidance on safer spending habits.

⚡ Key Features

  • 📈 Inflation tracking
  • 💰 Budget planner
  • 🛒 Smart shopping suggestions
  • 📊 Future spending predictions
  • 🧾 Savings recommendations
